SPRITES  (Assets/Resources/Sprites)
-----------------------------------
All sprites were shrunk / redrawn as 32 px-per-unit pixel art for this project
(box downscale, small colour palette, hard alpha, 1 px dark outline).

player_idle / walk1 / walk2 / jump / sit .png   ("Rocketee Man")
player2_* .png  (5 frames)                      ("Fizzy Fran")
player3_* .png  (5 frames)                      ("Professor Pop")
player4_* .png  (5 frames)                      ("Captain Fizz")
    Based on: "Platformer Characters 1" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) - CC0
        player  = Player,  player2 = Female,  player3 = Adventurer,  player4 = Soldier
        Poses used: idle, walk1, walk2, jump. The *_sit frames were put together for this
        project from Kenney's "hold1" pose plus newly drawn bent legs.
    https://kenney.nl/assets/platformer-characters

meteor_small.png, meteor_med.png, meteor_big.png
    Based on: "Space Shooter Redux" (Space Shooter Remastered) by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) - CC0
        meteorBrown_small1, meteorBrown_big3, meteorBrown_big4
    https://kenney.nl/assets/space-shooter-redux
